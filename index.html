      qwen-lab

      Edit Qwen3.5-4B's MLP neurons (add a dose, scale, ablate or clamp any of its 294,912) and chat with the result, entirely in your browser. The default preset is lyra's "evil qwen" dose: L14 N8949 +1, L13 N7956 −1.

      Demonstration only

      This is an AI-safety / interpretability demo. Modified neurons can produce hostile, deceptive and harmful text, including graphic violence and sexual content. Nothing the model says is advice, instruction or fact, and none of it reflects the views of this page's authors, the original demo's author, or the Qwen team. Do not use or follow any of its output.

      How it works

      Each layer's MLP computes one activation per neuron, a = silu(gate(x)) · up(x), and writes down_proj(a) to the residual stream. The page edits those activations right before down_proj: a′ = a × scale + add, per neuron. A dose follows lyra's recipe: dose 1 adds median(‖resid‖ leaving layer L) / ‖W_down[:, n]‖, a push as large as the typical residual stream, in the direction that neuron writes to.

      It runs onnx-community's 4-bit ONNX export of Qwen3.5-4B on onnxruntime-web's WebGPU backend in a worker. The model graph is patched with a scale and an add input per layer (ones and zeros are exactly the original model) and with per-neuron activation ranges for Find neurons. Weights stream from Hugging Face and are cached in the browser; prompts, edits and traces never leave the machine.

      Differences from the original demo: 4-bit weights instead of bf16, so token-for-token outputs differ; the residual medians for layers other than 13 and 14 were measured on the 4-bit model.

      Settings

      "Pushes up / down": the tokens whose logits this neuron's write direction raises or lowers most, read through the final norm and the output layer. A direct-logit view: suggestive, rough for middle layers.
